# Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our standard / 我们的标准

We welcome technically rigorous, respectful collaboration across maritime operations, simulation, optimization, reinforcement learning, safety engineering and governance. Participants are expected to:

- discuss people and ideas with respect, including when findings conflict;
- distinguish measured, public, replayed, derived and simulated data;
- disclose limitations, uncertainty, conflicts of interest and relevant affiliations;
- protect credentials, personal data and non-public operational information;
- make reviews specific, reproducible and proportionate to the risk.

我们欢迎围绕港航运营、仿真、优化、强化学习、安全工程和治理开展严谨且相互尊重的协作。参与者应尊重他人与不同观点，明确区分实测、公开、回放、派生和仿真数据，主动披露局限与利益关系，保护凭证、个人信息和非公开运营数据，并使评审意见具体、可复现且与风险相称。

## Unacceptable conduct / 不可接受的行为

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexualized attention, credential disclosure, deliberate unsafe guidance, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, and knowingly presenting simulation or derived data as measured production evidence are not acceptable. Attempts to bypass human approval, safety interlocks or fail-closed controls in a real operational environment are also out of scope.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、跟踪、人肉搜索、性暗示、泄露凭证、故意提供不安全指导、抄袭、伪造证据，以及明知是仿真或派生数据却宣称为生产实测证据，均不可接受。任何试图在真实运营环境中绕过人工确认、安全联锁或失败关闭控制的行为也不属于本项目允许的协作范围。

## Scope and enforcement / 适用范围与执行

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, project events and public representation of the project. Report concerns privately through the repository's supported private contact channel; security-sensitive matters must use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting. Do not post confidential evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, pause discussions, restrict participation or refer severe matters to the relevant platform. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

本准则适用于仓库讨论、代码评审、项目活动以及以项目名义进行的公开表达。请通过仓库支持的私密渠道报告问题；涉及安全的信息必须使用 GitHub 私密漏洞报告，禁止在公开 Issue 中提交机密证据。维护者可按风险程度编辑或移除内容、关闭贡献、暂停讨论、限制参与，严重情况可移交平台处理，并应在保护当事人隐私的前提下留存处置记录。

## Good-faith research / 善意研究

Good-faith vulnerability research is welcome when it follows [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), minimizes access and impact, avoids persistence or exfiltration, and gives maintainers a reasonable remediation window.

遵守 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)、尽量减少访问与影响、不建立持久化、不外传数据，并给维护者合理修复时间的善意安全研究受到欢迎。

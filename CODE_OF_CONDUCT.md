# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, precise and evidence-driven. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate unsafe guidance, credential disclosure and knowingly presenting simulated or derived data as measured data are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's supported private channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect people, systems and confidential operational information. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented privately.
